Interstellar (2014)

A. Introduction

Interstellar is a science fiction movie released in 2014. It was directed by Christopher Nolan and tells a story about space exploration, survival, and the love between family members. The movie shows how humans search for another planet where they can live because Earth is slowly becoming uninhabitable.

Genre: Science Fiction, Adventure, Drama
Release Year: 2014

---

B. Synopsis

The story follows Joseph Cooper, a former pilot and engineer who lives with his children on Earth. Due to environmental problems, the planet is slowly becoming unable to support human life. Cooper discovers a secret NASA mission that aims to find another habitable planet for humanity.

He joins a group of astronauts and travels through a wormhole to explore different planets. During their journey, they face dangerous situations and difficult decisions. Meanwhile, Cooper's daughter Murphy stays on Earth and works to solve the problem of saving humanity.

As the story continues, Cooper discovers that time and gravity play an important role in their mission. The movie shows how far people are willing to go to protect their loved ones and save the future of humanity.

C. Characters

1. Joseph Cooper – A former NASA pilot who joins the mission to find a new home for humanity.
2. Murphy Cooper – Cooper's intelligent daughter who works to solve the gravity problem.
3. Amelia Brand – A scientist and astronaut who joins Cooper on the space mission.
4. Professor Brand – A NASA scientist who leads the mission to save humanity.
5. Tom Cooper – Cooper's son who remains on Earth and takes care of the family.

D. Personal Review

I chose Interstellar because it is an interesting movie that combines science, adventure, and emotions. It makes me think about space, time, and the future of humanity.

Things I Liked About the Movie

- The amazing visual effects and space scenes.
- The interesting story about time and space exploration.
- The emotional connection between Cooper and his daughter.
- The background music that makes important scenes more meaningful.

My Rating: 10/10

I would recommend this movie to other people, especially those who enjoy science fiction and adventure movies. It is worth watching because it has an interesting story and teaches viewers about family, sacrifice, and hope.
